On October 2, 2026, at around 20:20 UTC, a network outage at our hosting provider, Worldstream, briefly made our services unreachable and interrupted analytics data processing.

Worldstream confirmed that a small number of racks in their network lost connectivity. They detected the interruption immediately and restored connectivity within five minutes. Our services recovered, and all related alerts cleared.

The incident is resolved. We're sorry for the disruption.
